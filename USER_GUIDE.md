# HireBoard — User Guide

**Live site:** https://candidate-screening-platform.netlify.app

This guide explains how to use the platform from both sides: as a **Recruiter** (managing jobs and applications) and as a **Candidate** (applying for jobs and tracking status).

---

## For Recruiters

### 1. Create an account
1. Go to the site → click **"Recruiter Login"** (top right)
2. Click **"Create an account"**
3. Fill in your name, email, and a password (at least 6 characters)
4. You'll be logged in automatically and taken to your dashboard

### 2. Log in (if you already have an account)
1. Click **"Recruiter Login"**
2. Enter your email and password

### 3. Post a new job
1. From your dashboard, click **"+ Post a Job"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Job title** (e.g. "Backend Software Engineer")
   - **Description** (responsibilities, requirements, etc.)
   - **Location** (optional, e.g. "Remote" or "Dhaka, Bangladesh")
   - **Employment type** (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, or Internship)
3. Click **"Post Job"**
4. Your job immediately appears on the public "Open Positions" page for candidates to see and apply to

### 4. View your jobs
Your dashboard ("Your Jobs") lists every job you've posted, showing:
- Current status (Open / Closed)
- Number of applications received
- Quick actions: Review Applications, Edit, Close/Reopen

### 5. Edit a job
Click **"Edit"** on any job card → update the fields → **"Save Changes"**

### 6. Close a job
Click **"Close Job"** on any open job card. This immediately stops it from accepting new applications and removes it from the public jobs list (candidates who already applied are unaffected — you can still review them).

You can **reopen** a closed job at any time using the same button.

### 7. Review applications
1. Click **"Review Applications"** on any job card
2. You'll see a table of every candidate who applied: name, email, a link to their resume, application date, and current status

### 8. Update a candidate's status
On the applications table, use the **status dropdown** next to each candidate to move them through the pipeline:
```
Applied → Shortlisted → Interview → Hired / Rejected
```
The change saves instantly — no separate save button needed.

### 9. Log out
Click **"Log out"** at the bottom of the sidebar.

## For Candidates

Candidates don't need an account — just a valid email address.

### 1. Browse open jobs
Go to the site — the homepage shows every currently open position with its title, location, employment type, and a short description.

### 2. View full job details
Click **"View & Apply"** on any job to see the full description.

### 3. Apply for a job
On the job details page, fill in the application form:
- **Full name**
- **Email address** — use an email you'll remember; you'll need it to check your status later
- **Resume URL** — a link to your resume (Google Drive, Dropbox, personal portfolio, or LinkedIn profile). File uploads aren't supported — only links.

Click **"Submit Application"**. You'll see a confirmation message once it's received.

**Note:** you can only apply once per job per email address — submitting a second application with the same email for the same job will be rejected.

### 4. Track your application status
1. Click **"Track Application"** in the top navigation
2. Enter the email address you applied with
3. Click **"Check Status"**

You'll see a table of every job you've applied to and its current status:
| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Applied** | Your application has been received |
| **Shortlisted** | The recruiter has shortlisted you for further review |
| **Interview** | You've been moved to the interview stage |
| **Hired** | Congratulations — you got the job! |
| **Rejected** | The recruiter has decided not to move forward |

You can check this page anytime, as often as you like, using the same email.
